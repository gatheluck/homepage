Yoshihiro Fukuhara (aka Gatheluck) is a Research Engineer at CADDi, specializing in Computer Vision and Machine Learning.

His research interests include adversarial robustness, neural network verification, computer vision, and MLOps. He is also a Specially Appointed Fellow at AIST and a Research Fellow at Waseda University.

Previously, he worked as an ML/MLOps Engineer at ExaWizards Inc, where he developed and deployed machine learning systems for various applications. He completed his doctoral coursework at Waseda University in the Department of Applied Physics.
